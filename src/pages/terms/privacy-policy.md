### 1.2 What Data Is Collected Automatically (Server Logs)

The Site is hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**. When you visit the Site, Cloudflare and my hosting infrastructure
may automatically log the following information for **security and service-availability purposes only**:

- Request timestamp and the URL you accessed
- HTTP referer (the previous page that linked to the Site)
- Browser User-Agent string
- General geographic location (country / region / city level, **not** a precise physical address)
- An IP address (stored briefly for DDoS / bot mitigation, and is never used for user profiling)

This log data is **never** sold, shared with third parties, or used to build individual user profiles.
Retention period: raw access logs are rotated / deleted within 30 days. Aggregated, anonymised statistics
(such as total pageviews per month) may be kept indefinitely for internal performance review.

### 1.3 Web Analytics

The Site uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to understand overall traffic patterns in a privacy-preserving
manner. Cloudflare Web Analytics does **not** use tracking cookies and does **not** fingerprint individual
visitors. Only aggregated metrics such as "total unique visitors per day" and "top visited URLs" are
reported. Please refer to
[Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for further details.

No other third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Baidu Tongji, etc.) are installed on this Site.

### 1.4 Comment Functionality

> **Status at the time of this policy (August 2026): commenting features are temporarily disabled.**
> The following paragraphs describe how user data would be handled **if and when** comments are re-enabled
> in the future. Should I change the comment provider or data handling, this Privacy Policy will be
> updated and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

If comment functionality is enabled in the future, the Site plans to use **Waline** (self-hosted comment
system). When you submit a comment you may be asked to provide:

- Display name / nickname (required)
- Email address (optional, used only to reply to your comment; it will **never** be displayed publicly or
  shared with third parties)
- Personal website / URL (optional, displayed as a link next to your nickname)

All comment data is stored in the self-hosted Waline database operated by the Site Operator and is never
shared with third parties. You may request permanent deletion of any comment you have submitted at any
time by sending an email to the address listed in §1.1. Requests will usually be handled within 7 working
days.

### 1.5 Cookies and Local Storage

The Site uses only **functional, first-party storage** – no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are
used. Specifically, the following data may be stored locally via cookies or `localStorage`:

- User-selected colour theme (light / dark mode)
- User-selected language preference on certain bilingual pages (e.g. the About page)
- A Cloudflare Turnstile verification token (short-lived, used only for anti-bot protection during the
  current browser session)

No personally identifiable information is written into cookies, and cookies are **never** used to track
your browsing behaviour across third-party sites.

### 1.6 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

1. Request a copy of any personal data I hold about you;
2. Request correction of inaccurate or out-of-date personal data;
3. Request permanent erasure of personal data (e.g. a comment you submitted);
4. Object to or restrict certain kinds of processing;
5. Withdraw consent you previously provided at any time (withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of
   processing carried out before withdrawal).

To exercise any of these rights, please send a signed email to the contact address in §1.1. I will
respond within 30 days.

### 1.7 Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When material changes are made, the "Last updated"
date at the top of this page will be revised and a brief notice may be posted on the About page or the
Site's home page. Your continued use of the Site after such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised
policy.
